About the project

An Independent American Mahjong Exploration

Play Mahjong Craft is an independent project focused on learning the American Mahjong market, presenting thoughtfully selected style directions and connecting genuine inquiries with workable production options.

The website is currently an inquiry and market-validation platform rather than an online store. The imagery is used as design reference material. Before any project is accepted, product availability, image usage, specifications, supplier capability, samples, pricing, quality expectations and delivery terms need to be confirmed.

The aim is simple: communicate honestly, learn what real buyers value and develop reliable product directions one project at a time.

Questions before you inquire

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the styles shown ready to ship?

No ready-to-ship inventory is implied. The styles are design references for inquiry and customization discussions. Availability, specifications, sample options, pricing and production timing are confirmed for each project.

Can I order a sample?

Sample availability and cost depend on the selected style and level of customization. Send the style, destination country and intended quantity for confirmation.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by material, artwork, packaging and manufacturing method. Standard-style projects may support lower quantities than fully customized projects.

Can tile artwork and packaging be customized?

Potential options may include tile artwork, colors, backs, logos, cases, gift boxes and selected accessories. Feasibility is confirmed after your requirements are reviewed.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on whether a sample is needed, the amount of customization, project quantity and destination. An estimated schedule is provided only after the requirements are clear.

How do I receive a quotation?

Share the preferred style, intended use, quantity, destination country and customization requirements through WhatsApp. A quotation can follow after feasibility and supplier pricing are confirmed.
