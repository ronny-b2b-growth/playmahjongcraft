Samples, MOQ & timing

Why These Answers Depend on the Project

Samples: A reference-style sample and a newly customized sample are different routes. Cost and timing depend on artwork, material and packaging requirements.

MOQ: Standard directions may support lower quantities than new molds, complex printing, custom packaging or a full private-label program.

Lead time: The schedule depends on artwork approval, sample needs, quantity, production capacity and destination. A date is estimated only after the brief is reviewed.

Custom set questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What can be customized on an American Mahjong set?

Potential options include tile faces, Jokers, colors, backs, logos, cases, gift boxes, inserts and selected accessories. Feasibility depends on the artwork, material, manufacturing method and quantity.

Can I request a sample before production?

Yes, sample routes can be reviewed before production. Availability, sample cost and timing depend on whether the request uses a reference style or requires new artwork and packaging.

What is the MOQ for custom American Mahjong sets?

Minimum order quantity varies by material, artwork, packaging and production method. Standard-style projects may support lower quantities than fully customized or private-label projects.

Are the pictured Mahjong sets in stock?

No ready-to-ship inventory is implied. The images are visual references for project discussions. Current availability and specifications are confirmed for each inquiry.

Can I use my club or company logo?

Logo placement may be considered for tile artwork, packaging, inserts or selected accessories. Share a vector logo if available so the intended placement and production method can be reviewed.

What details are needed for a quotation?

Share the intended use, preferred style, estimated quantity, destination country, artwork or logo needs, packaging requirements and target timing. A quotation follows after feasibility and current supplier pricing are confirmed.
